# TSC annual-report reference fixture

The upgraded parser was tested locally against this report and extracted:

- Company: TSC Ltd
- Financial years: 2025 and 2024
- Revenue from operations: 5,590 and 5,200 crore
- Net profit/PAT: 408 and 338 crore
- Total assets: 5,650 and 5,180 crore
- Total borrowings: 1,920 and 1,810 crore
- Total shareholders' equity: 2,400 and 2,150 crore
- Net cash from operating activities: 388 and 330 crore
- FINScore: 72/100 (Moderate Risk)

This fixture exercises common annual-report aliases such as `Turnover`, `PAT`, `Net worth`, `Borrowings`, `Revenue from operations`, and `Net cash from operating activities`.
